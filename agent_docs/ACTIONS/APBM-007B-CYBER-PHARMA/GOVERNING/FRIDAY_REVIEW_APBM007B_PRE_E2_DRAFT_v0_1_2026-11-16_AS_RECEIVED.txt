## Additions for the frozen pack

Format: ID; target; concern; exact addition; owner.

**PE-01 — Removal must not create a refund or credit.**
- Target: AR-04.
- Concern: AR-04 describes the proposal as lowering the quantity immediately while access continues to period end. Lowering a Stripe quantity mid-period can raise a proration credit. Decisions record 5.11 says the store's charge stops at the end of the paid period, and 5.14 excludes refunds in the first release.
- Addition to AR-04: "A scheduled removal produces no credit, refund or proration line in the current period. The charge stops from the next period. Prove with the provider's invoice preview and the next real invoice."
- Owner: Architect; evidence from the probe.

**PE-02 — Transfer identity: reviewer input for the Director's choice.**
- Target: AR-05 and the Director request.
- Concern: AR-05 correctly sends the identity model to the Director. Two points should be in front of him when he chooses.
  1. The Director ruled that a buyer gets no access to past claims. A separate buyer store record makes that denial structural: the buyer's record has no history attached. A single shared record makes it a policy enforced by filters, which is harder to prove and easier to break.
  2. Whatever the model, the rule "no two stores share an NPI" (decisions record 6.1, 6.3) must survive as "no two active stores share an NPI", enforced by the database, with the duplicate-NPI-goes-to-support behaviour and the concurrent-reservation protection from 007A unchanged.
- Addition: put both points in the Director request, and add to the affected criterion: "At most one active store per NPI at any moment, including during a transfer; proved with two concurrent attempts."
- Owner: Architect drafts; Director decides.

**PE-03 — Name the test-clock work as the first candidate for a bounded checkpoint.**
- Target: checklist, "Smooth E2 execution requirements"; AR-06.
- Concern: the probe excludes test clocks. O-1 is therefore the one design frozen without capability evidence.
- Addition: "O-1 (live failed-renewal with a test clock) is frozen either on probe evidence, or with one exact engineering checkpoint and a fully specified fallback. It is not left as 'choose later'."
- Owner: Architect.

**PE-04 — Director-accepted screens enter the regression list.**
- Target: checklist rows "Baseline" and "QAM"; AC-028.
- Concern: the brief keeps the certified-versus-accepted distinction but does not add the screens to regression. U-1, U-2 and U-3 were merged without QA.
- Addition to AC-028: "Regression includes the navigation highlight, the OwedBook billing fields and the password show/hide controls on every screen 007B touches."
- Owner: Architect.

**PE-05 — Rebind the QA inputs to what E1 found.**

**PE-07 — Operator identity: reviewer recommendation.**
- Target: AR-09 and the Director request.
- Recommendation: for staging, a shared operator credential with a mandatory named-operator field recorded in the audit row, stated on the certificate as a known limitation. Individual operator sign-in is required before production. The audit row must separate "authenticated as" from "declared actor".
- Owner: Director decides.

- Recommendation: the sender domain already verified for sign-in mail is the natural sender for billing notices, sent through the provider's own sending interface, not through the sign-in mail path. The frozen contract names the one service that sends them.
- Owner: Architect proposes; Director confirms the sender.
