## Amendments

Format: ID; file; concern; exact change; stage; owner.

### Before E2 release

**BP-01 — A quote below the provider's minimum charge breaks the mechanism.**
- Files: `CONTRACTS/BILLING.md` (Incremental charge; Expiry); `ACCEPTANCE_SPEC.md` AC-003; `EXECUTION_PLAN.md` CP-1.
- Concern: the contract refuses confirmation only in the last 90 minutes of a period. The remaining-period price of a 4,900-cent store falls below 50 cents roughly seven and a half hours before a monthly period ends. Stripe's documentation states that an invoice below the minimum chargeable amount is marked paid without a charge and the amount is moved to the customer's balance as a debit for the next invoice (docs.stripe.com/billing/customer/balance, transaction type `invoice_too_small`; docs.stripe.com/invoicing/overview). Under this contract that produces a "paid" invoice with nothing paid, an amount mismatch, an attention state, and a non-zero balance that then blocks every later Add Store for that account.
- Exact change:
  1. BILLING, after the formula: "If the computed amount is below 50 cents (the provider's minimum charge for USD, read from one named constant), refuse the quote and the confirmation with a plain message that the store can be added after renewal. No invoice is created. This refusal replaces the 90-minute rule wherever it is stricter."
  2. AC-003, append: "A quote below the provider's minimum charge is refused before any invoice exists; no customer balance is created."
  3. CP-1, append: "Confirm the sub-minimum refusal by computation only; do not create a sub-minimum invoice."
- Owner: Architect.

**BP-02 — Removal and undo have no protection at the period boundary.**
- Files: `CONTRACTS/LIFECYCLE_AND_TRANSFER.md` (Scheduled removal); `CONTRACTS/BILLING.md` (Quantity/access accounting); AC-019.
- Concern: Add Store is refused near the boundary. Removal and undo are not. A removal whose quantity change lands after the renewal invoice is created bills the removed store for another period. An undo that lands after it leaves a store active in a period it was not billed for. AC-019 names overcharge only.
- Exact change:
  1. LIFECYCLE: "Scheduling a removal and undoing one are refused inside the same end-of-period window as Add Store. Each binds the subscription period it was requested in. After the provider quantity change, re-read the subscription; if the period is no longer the bound one, the operation enters attention and no store is left active unbilled or billed after its cutoff."
  2. AC-019, replace "cannot silently overcharge or reactivate a store" with "cannot silently overcharge, undercharge or reactivate a store; a store is never active in a period whose renewal invoice excluded it".
- An equivalent rule that achieves the same outcome is acceptable if the Architect records it.
- Owner: Architect.

**BP-03 — When the buyer may pay in a transfer is not stated.**
- Files: `CONTRACTS/LIFECYCLE_AND_TRANSFER.md` (Sale history); AC-022.
- Concern: the contract says buyer-paid activation closes the seller's ownership in one transaction, and also that the seller keeps access until the agreed effective time. If the buyer pays early, one of those two statements must give way. The recon proposal reserved the NPI for the buyer "from the effective time"; the frozen text dropped that phrase.
- Exact change, LIFECYCLE: "A buyer quote or confirmation for a transfer-reserved NPI is refused before the transfer's effective time. From the effective time, the reservation holds the NPI for the exact buyer until the authorization's recorded expiry. Between the effective time and buyer activation the pharmacy has no active ownership. Reservation expiry without buyer payment enters attention; the NPI is never released to the public by timeout."
- AC-022, append: "A buyer cannot activate before the effective time; the seller's access does not end early."
- Owner: Architect.

**BP-04 — A paid operation that will never be activated has no end state.**
- Files: `CONTRACTS/BILLING.md` (Expiry and late payment; States); `CONTRACTS/LIFECYCLE_AND_TRANSFER.md` (operator commands); `SUPPORT_PROCEDURES.md`; AC-007.
- Concern: a late payment goes to attention with no automatic refund, and resumes only if the owner reconfirms. Unresolved states hold the account's single operation slot and the NPI hold. If the owner does not want the store any more, the account can never add or remove a store again, and the money has no recorded outcome. Refunds are outside the app by decisions record 5.9 and 5.14, so the software needs a way to record that one happened.
- Exact change:
  1. BILLING, States: add `closed_refunded_externally` (terminal).
  2. Operator commands: add `store-operation-close` with exact operation ID, actor, reason and ticket. It requires an authoritative provider read showing the operation's invoice fully refunded or credited. Only then does it set the terminal state and release the NPI hold and the operation slot. It never issues a refund and never activates. Without that proof it refuses.
  3. SUPPORT_PROCEDURES: add the procedure — who decides, who performs the refund in the provider dashboard, the evidence reference, the close command.
  4. AC-007, append: "A paid operation that is not activated ends only by owner-reconfirmed activation or by operator close on provider proof of refund; the account is never left permanently blocked."
- Owner: Architect.

**BP-05 — `removed-store-reactivate` is named but not defined.**
- Files: `CONTRACTS/LIFECYCLE_AND_TRANSFER.md`; `CONTRACTS/API_ROLE_MATRIX.md` W4.
- Concern: the command appears in the list with no rule for payment, quantity or history. Undefined, it is a path to a store that is active and not billed.
- Exact change, LIFECYCLE: "`removed-store-reactivate` authorizes only. It applies to the account that owned the removed store, and refuses if the NPI has an active ownership or the store was transferred. The owner then pays through the ordinary Add Store flow at the remaining-period price. The command never changes provider quantity and never grants access."
- One Director default is needed and is listed below: whether the re-added store is the original record with its history, or a new one.
- Owner: Architect; Director for the default.
