# Stale-spec mapping and retirement (P-09, AC-R group; plan step 8)

**Ruling:** P-09 approved by AM-08 (1): Engineering maps each spec that targets the removed `/demo` routes to current coverage, retires those with no current route, and does not rewrite them. Independent QA builds current-route checks in its own lane; retirement alone does not close the regression gap (AC-R04 does).

**Evidence that the routes are gone.** Current Portal page routes (from `find src/app -name page.tsx`, route groups stripped): `/auth/confirm`, `/auth/handoff-recovery`, `/auth/link-expired`, `/auth/signed-out`, `/auth/sign-in-to-continue`, `/auth/switch`, `/billing`, `/dashboard`, `/login`, `/register/activation`, `/register/owner`, `/register/pharmacy`, `/register/plan`, `/register/review`, `/stores/add`. `find src/app -path '*demo*'` returns nothing, and no `/demo` path appears in `src/app` or `src/proxy.ts`. Every one of the specs below navigates to `/demo/...` (directly or through route arrays at the top of the file), so its suite cannot reach its screens on the candidate.

**What retirement means here.** The 16 files are removed from the working tree; their baseline content stays in Git at `cb4decd` (blob shown). `playwright.config.ts` is protected and was not edited; its projects simply match fewer files. Nothing was rewritten or re-pointed.

**Specs kept:** `apbm007b.addstore.spec.ts`, `apbm007b.regression.spec.ts`, `apbm007b.o1-state.spec.ts` (current routes). They still drive a dark theme through `e2e/gate-m.ts` (plan PP-3); QA decides in Q1 whether they are usable instruments.

**Tests inside retired files that touch current routes too** (not rewritten, listed for QA's current-route lane): `public.boundaries.spec.ts` ("session expired banner via enum only", `/login?reason=`), `account.journeys.spec.ts` and `account.repair.spec.ts` (dashboard tests that also open `/demo` references), `logout.final.spec.ts` (`/dashboard` sign-out that starts from `/demo/register/plan`), `public.zoom.spec.ts` (zoom check over a route list that includes `/demo` routes).
