1. Getting around

Pharmacy Books has two parts that work together.

  • OwedBook is where you work day to day: your pharmacies, your staff, settings and the audit log.
  • Account & Billing is where the account owner manages the subscription: adding a pharmacy, payment details and invoices.

You sign in once. Moving from one part to the other does not ask you to sign in again.

Payment details and invoices are handled on Stripe's secure billing page. When you finish there, use the return link at the top of the Stripe page to come back to Account & Billing.

Things to know

  • There is one Owner for the account. The Owner is the person who pays for the subscription.
  • Making someone a Store admin does not hand over the account or the billing. The Owner keeps full access.
  • The Owner cannot be removed from a pharmacy by a Store admin.
  • If a Store admin opens a pharmacy they are not assigned to, a short message explains that, with a link back to My Stores. A Member who opens a My Stores page is taken to the OwedBook page.

3. My Stores

What it does

Lists every pharmacy you manage.

Who can use it

Owners and Store admins. Members do not have My Stores; they choose a pharmacy with the Store selector at the top of the OwedBook page.

How to use it

  1. 1Step 1: Open Admin Portal from the menu. My Stores is its first page (open My Stores).
  2. 2Step 2: Each pharmacy shows as a card with its status.
  3. 3Step 3: Open a pharmacy to see its people.

What you should see

Owners see every pharmacy on the account. Store admins see only the pharmacies they were invited to. A new pharmacy starts with no claims; that is expected.

4. Inviting staff

What it does

Gives a person access to one pharmacy.

Who can use it

The Owner, or the Store admin of that pharmacy.

How to do it

  1. 1Step 1: Open My Stores and open the pharmacy.
  2. 2Step 2: Choose Invite member.
  3. 3Step 3: Enter the person's email address.
  4. 4Step 4: Choose their role: Member or Store admin. The role you choose decides what they can do. Their email address does not. The Store admin choice appears only when you are allowed to appoint one.
  5. 5Step 5: Choose Send invitation.

What should happen

The person receives an email with a sign-in link. Until they accept, the invitation is listed under Invitations as pending. After they accept, they appear under People with access and see only that pharmacy.

If you chose the wrong role

Invite them again with the correct role. The earlier invitation stays in the history.

Resending or revoking an invitation

While an invitation is pending, you can choose Resend or Revoke next to it in the Invitations list. The person you invited can also decline it from their invitations page.

5. Changing a store admin

What it does

Lets someone else manage staff for one pharmacy.

Who can use it

The Owner.

How to do it

  1. 1Step 1: Open the pharmacy from My Stores and choose Invite member.
  2. 2Step 2: Invite the person with the role Store admin. If the pharmacy already has a Store admin, the form asks what should happen to the current one.
  3. 3Step 3: They accept the invitation from their email.

What should happen

The new Store admin can invite and remove Members for that pharmacy. The Owner still sees every pharmacy and still controls billing. The Store admin cannot remove the Owner and cannot see billing details.

6. Removing someone's access

What it does

Takes away a Member's access to one pharmacy.

Who can use it

The Owner, or the Store admin of that pharmacy.

How to do it

  1. 1Step 1: Open the pharmacy from My Stores and choose Invite member; the people for that pharmacy are listed there.
  2. 2Step 2: Find the person under People with access and choose Remove access.
  3. 3Step 3: Read the confirmation. It names the person and the pharmacy.
  4. 4Step 4: Choose Remove access to confirm, or Cancel to leave things as they are.

What should happen

If you cancel, nothing changes. If you confirm, the person can still sign in but no longer sees that pharmacy. Other people in the pharmacy are not affected. The removal is recorded in the audit log. The Owner and a Store admin are not removed this way.

7. Billing and adding a pharmacy

Who can use it

The Owner only. Store admins see a short message that billing is managed by the account owner.

The price

The first pharmacy is $99.00 a month. Each additional pharmacy is $49.00 a month. Any tax or approved discount is shown before you pay.

To see your subscription

  1. 1Step 1: In OwedBook, open Admin Portal, then Billing (open Billing), then choose Manage billing. You arrive in Account & Billing, already signed in.
  2. 2Step 2: Your plan, your pharmacies and your monthly total are shown, with two counts: Pharmacies with access now and Billed at next renewal. They differ only when a pharmacy has been scheduled for removal.
  3. 3Step 3: To change your card or see invoices, choose Manage billing in Account & Billing to open the Stripe billing page. Use the return link on the Stripe page when you are done.

To add a pharmacy

  1. 1Step 1: In Account & Billing, choose Add a pharmacy. (In OwedBook, Add store on the Billing page takes you there.)
  2. 2Step 2: Enter the pharmacy's name and its NPI. The NPI is required and must not already be registered. NCPDP is optional.
  3. 3Step 3: Review the price. You will see the amount due today and your new monthly total. The amount due today covers the rest of the current billing period, so it changes from day to day.
  4. 4Step 4: Tick the confirmation and pay.

What should happen

When the payment succeeds, the new pharmacy shows as active in both OwedBook and Account & Billing, and your invoice appears in your billing history. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, follow its prompt.

If the payment does not go through

The pharmacy is not added and your plan does not change. Check the message on screen before trying again. Do not pay twice for the same pharmacy.

8. Removing a pharmacy

What it does

Stops billing for one pharmacy from the next renewal.

Who can use it

The Owner.

How to do it

  1. 1Step 1: In Account & Billing, find the pharmacy and choose Remove at period end.
  2. 2Step 2: Read the confirmation. It shows the date access ends.
  3. 3Step 3: Confirm, or cancel to leave things as they are.

What should happen

The pharmacy stays fully usable until the date shown. Billed at next renewal goes down by one. Pharmacies with access now does not change yet.

Changed your mind?

Any time before that date, choose Keep this store. The removal is cancelled and your renewal count goes back up.

Removing one pharmacy is not the same as cancelling your whole subscription, which affects every pharmacy on the account. Cancelling the subscription is done on the Stripe billing page, and takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

9. Pharmacy settings

What it does

Shows the details saved for each pharmacy.

Who can use it

The Owner, for every pharmacy. A Store admin, for their own pharmacies.

How to use it

  1. 1Step 1: Open Admin Portal, then Settings.
  2. 2Step 2: Choose a pharmacy from the list.

What you should see

The saved details, including the NPI. These details are read-only in this release. A field that was never filled in shows "Not provided". To correct a saved detail, contact support.

10. Audit log

What it does

Shows who did what, to which pharmacy, and when.

Who can use it

The Owner, for every pharmacy. A Store admin, for their own pharmacies.

How to use it

  1. 1Step 1: Open Admin Portal, then Audit log.
  2. 2Step 2: Use Show to narrow the list: People and invitations, Pharmacies or Billing. All changes shows everything.
  3. 3Step 3: Turn on Include views and system steps to see more detail.
  4. 4Step 4: Choose Load older events to see earlier entries.

How to read an entry

Each entry shows the person, the action, the pharmacy and the time in your local time zone. An entry that says a removal was scheduled means the pharmacy is still active until the date shown. Some entries are made by the system, such as a payment update, and show no person's name.

11. Your profile and signing in

Your profile

Open Profile from the menu or the account menu. The page, My Profile, shows your name, email address and role. Your name and email are read-only here; to change them, contact support. You can set a new password on the same page.

Signing in

Enter your email address and we send you a sign-in link. Open the link, then press the button on the page to finish signing in. Each link works once. If you set a password, you can sign in with it instead.

If a link has expired or was already used

Ask for a new one from the sign-in page. The newest link is the one that works.

Signing out

Open the account menu (your initial at the top right) and choose Logout. On a phone, open Menu and choose Log out. In Account & Billing the same action is called Sign out. Signing out of OwedBook also ends your session in Account & Billing.

If you can sign in but see no pharmacies

Your access may have been removed, or your invitation may not be accepted yet. Ask your Owner or Store admin.
