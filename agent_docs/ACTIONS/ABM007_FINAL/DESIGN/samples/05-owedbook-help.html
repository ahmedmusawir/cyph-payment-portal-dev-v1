How to use OwedBook

Short answers for owners, store admins and members.

Invite a staff member

Who can do this: the Owner, or the Store admin of that pharmacy.

1

Open the pharmacy

Go to My Stores and open the pharmacy the person will work in.

2

Enter their email and choose a role

Pick Member or Store admin. The role you pick decides what they can do.

3

Send the invitation

They get an email with a sign-in link. They show as Pending until they accept.

What should happen:Once they accept, they appear in the pharmacy's people list and see only that pharmacy.
