# QA Lead ruling on J-e scope — 25 November 2026

Attributed to Friday, QA Lead. Relayed by Tony, Director, with his instruction to start J-e at his inbox.

"For AC-R04 (e), 'scoped to that one pharmacy' means the invitation
grants access to exactly one pharmacy, the one invited to. After
acceptance the Invitee sees its own pre-existing pharmacy plus that
one invited pharmacy, and no other Owner A pharmacy. The Invitee's
existing pharmacy and role there must be unchanged. Seeing any other
Owner A pharmacy, or losing the existing one, is a product failure."
